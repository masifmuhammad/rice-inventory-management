# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

999 is the user's friend's Pakistani rice company. Its public landing page introduces the company and provides direct access to the existing rice inventory software.

## Users

The public page serves visitors learning about 999. The existing authenticated application serves rice business staff managing stock, sales, cash, and reporting.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing React application and Express API already implement authenticated, permission-controlled inventory workflows. Preserve those workflows and active-business branding. The public page must have inventory access pills in its header and at its bottom. The user retired the orange pill colour on 22 October 2026; the pills are now near-black ink, turning brand vermilion on hover.

## Brand Commitments

Company name: 999. Haji Muhammad Rice Mills is the family’s main business, owned by the user's friend's father. The friend wants a distinct market presence for 999. Rejected: silver balloon lettering, the green/orange combination, and a serif display face (Fraunces) on the public page — keep Schibsted Grotesk.

Identity (22 October 2026 rebrand): the 999 taj — the crown panel of a Pakistani truck with a rice-grain finial and three nines whose counters are rice grains (`frontend/public/brand/999/logo-taj.svg`). Header lockup is the mark plus “Rice Company” only. Urdu appears only where it carries meaning (hero line, the truck motto, the journey ending, in-world signs and packs) and is set in Noto Sans Arabic to match Schibsted Grotesk; calligraphic Nastaliq and Gulzar were rejected as clashing. Palette: vermilion #E8322A, marigold #F4B400, cobalt #1F4FD8, truck-art pink #D93A7A on rice white #F4F1EA and ink #16130F.

Page structure, from the user's references: header and chapter dock after dmcg.co/work/archiv; a pinned scroll journey after unitedcarriers.com (field → sacks → painted truck → mill → pack → kiryana → dastarkhwan); a Recess-style rice range; a footer after footer.design with a truck-art jhalar fringe, the motto دیکھ مگر پیار سے (translation on hover or tap) and a dusk Punjab field scene. GSAP drives headline reveals, the hero intro and the family patchwork. Illustrations are drawn SVG stand-ins until the user's Codex artwork arrives; slots and prompts are in `999-images/ARTWORK-PROMPTS.md`. The first Codex batch (heavy truck-art on every object, glossy 3D, a dome-shaped logo) was rejected; prompts now refine the site's own exported drawings in `999-images/reference/`.

## Evidence on Hand

The user's screenshots, reference websites, existing application, and https://hmricemills.com/. The family website already describes 999 as an existing rice label. Build a fresh expression of this name, without inventing a founding story. The new 999 entity's contact details, exact range and certifications remain unconfirmed. Do not transfer parent-company performance claims to it.

## Open Decisions

The precise 999 product catalogue and contact details need confirmation before public launch. The user has now pinned the design direction: independent contemporary 999 identity, the Dastarkhwan direction (one white cloth seen from above), with the vermilion glyph and horseman as identity.
